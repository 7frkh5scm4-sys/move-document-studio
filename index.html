Move Document Studio

Version 2 · Enlarge and split PDFs without rebuilding their text.

Documents stay in your browser. Internet access loads the third-party libraries. No direct reMarkable connection. Keep originals and check your output.

PDF margins

Uses a low-resolution visual scan. Near-white details, annotations, and faint content may be missed. If content is missing, turn automatic trimming off and use manual margins. Detected bounds include a small safety allowance.

Manual percentages trim the original visible page first. Automatic detection, if selected, looks within that remaining area.

Enlarge and split

At 100%, trimmed content fills the available page width. Higher zoom also creates left-to-right tiles so no side content is discarded. Reading order is across each row, then down. Overlap is vertical; horizontal tiles have a small fixed overlap. Splits can cross text, images, or tables.

Reading layout

Split mode retains PDF page artwork, text, and images, not interactive features. It does not preserve annotations, links, forms, bookmarks, or digital signatures in the new PDF. Flatten annotations in another tool first if they matter. Reading mode is text-only; tables, footnotes, columns, and formatting may change. No OCR. Rotated PDF pages are not supported in split mode.
