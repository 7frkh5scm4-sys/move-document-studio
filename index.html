Move Document Studio

Version 3.2 · RTF images · RTF: 150 MB · PDF/DOCX: 40 MB

Files are processed in your browser. Internet loads third-party libraries. No document-conversion upload endpoint. Large files can exhaust memory; limits are not tested device capacities.

Manual trimming precedes automatic detection. Faint content may be missed. Disable automatic detection if needed.

Pictures retain source-stream order and fit within pages without stretching. Original wrapping, tables, rich formatting, and caption grouping are not retained. Unsupported or failed picture groups are reported. DOCX/PDF reformatting remains text-only.

Prototype, not browser-tested. Keep originals and compare output. No OCR. Reconstructed PDFs omit annotations, interactive links, forms, bookmarks, tags, and signatures. Rotated sources are unsupported in reconstructed PDF modes. Splits can cross text/tables. RTF parsing is basic: WMF/EMF, binary/linked pictures, embedded objects, complex layouts, and non-Latin font coverage are unsupported. Large files may stall or crash the browser.
