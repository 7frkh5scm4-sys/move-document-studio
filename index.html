Move Document Studio

Version 3 · Fill-height tiles and continuous reading

Files are processed in your browser; this app has no document-upload endpoint. Internet is required for third-party libraries. Keep originals and inspect the output.

Manual trim is applied first. Automatic trim finds visible marks within the remaining region and adds a 5 pt safety allowance. Faint content can be missed; disable it if necessary.

At 100%, Continuous reading fits each trimmed source page to the output width. Fill-height mode scales each original page to at least the available output height and width, then tiles it. Larger zoom may require horizontal tiles. Tile order: left to right, then downward. Overlap applies to independent tiles; continuous reading uses no overlap to avoid duplicating text.

Continuous reading preserves the artwork inside each placed section, not original page boundaries. It does not remove internal whitespace or intelligently detect paragraphs. Splits can cross text or tables. Horizontal tiling preserves all side content but separates wide tables into sections. PDF annotations, interactive links, forms, bookmarks, tags, and signatures are not transferred into reconstructed output. Rotated source pages are unsupported for reconstructed output. Reading mode omits images and complex formatting; RTF support is basic. No OCR.
