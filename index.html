Move Document Studio

Prepare documents for your reMarkable Paper Pro Move.

Files are processed in this browser, not uploaded to a document server. Internet access is required to load the open-source libraries. This tool creates PDFs; it does not send them to reMarkable.

Reading layout changes pagination and may lose tables, images, footnote placement, columns, and formatting. Check the preview against the original. Scanned PDFs require OCR elsewhere. Cropping preserves PDF content but does not enlarge or reflow its text.

Reading pages use a 324 × 576 pt portrait canvas (9:16 proportions). This is a layout preset, not a requirement imposed by the device.

Preview

Preview shows up to the first 8 pages of the most recently prepared document. Export includes all pages.

Before you use it

  • DOCX: extracts paragraphs using Mammoth. Embedded images are not included in reading output.
  • RTF: basic text extraction only; complex RTF, non-Western encodings, objects, and nested formatting may not convert correctly.
  • PDF: best for simple, single-column documents with selectable text. Multi-column extraction may be out of order.
  • Preserve-layout mode accepts PDFs only. For DOCX/RTF layout fidelity, export a PDF from your word processor first.
  • Reading mode uses standard Latin fonts. Unsupported characters are replaced with question marks and reported.
  • Do not use this output as an authoritative copy for legal citations or page references.
